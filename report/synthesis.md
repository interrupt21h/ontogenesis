# What we learned, and what to try next {.unnumbered}

Ontogenesis began with local chemical rules and developed into a set of instruments for encountering increasingly complicated mathematical fields. Its generations are not stages in the growth of one organism. They are separate numerical investigations, each modifying a model, an experimental control or a way of observing the result. The visual sequence records an accumulating capacity to see the artificial world, as much as changes to the world itself.

## From patterns to surprisingly complicated worlds {.unnumbered}

In the earliest experiments, spatial differentiation appears without an explicit object model. Competition and transport alter how that differentiation persists and moves. Later systems add energetic constraints, environmental feedback, heredity and local structural material. These additions do not yield an inevitable ascent toward living organization: experiments can saturate the domain, erase threshold-defined features or reveal different structures depending on the chosen measurement.

The matrix comparison across G0016–G0018 is a useful example. Reported occupancy changes dramatically, but the models, durations and mechanisms also differ. The endpoint comparison is descriptive, not a controlled causal test. G0023 supplies a more discriminating matched-seed comparison of construction-related costs and protection. Its four treatment arms distinguish effects within that configuration while leaving cross-seed generality and the separate contributions of bundled costs unresolved.

G0024 encodes construction independently from several other inherited traits. At its recorded endpoint, mean matrix concentration is about 0.146, with roughly 50.04% coverage above threshold 0.15 but only four cells above threshold 0.20. G0025 introduces an ongoing energy debit for maintenance; it records mean matrix 0.13449 and no cells above 0.20. These observations resist simple descriptions of structure as present or absent. A visual field may be widespread at one threshold while providing little high-concentration material at another.

## Watching the thing without changing it {.unnumbered}

Higher-dimensional simulation makes comprehensive visual inspection impossible. A three-dimensional section samples only part of a four-dimensional state. Projection, camera motion, light accumulation and display interpolation can communicate spatial form, but they can also conceal the values and locations from which the image is made. Visual clarity must therefore be accompanied by numerical fidelity and explicit observational limits.

G0026 verifies, at 41 sampled checkpoints in the tested execution, that its read-only observer does not change the computed dynamic fields. G0027 adds spectral and adaptive-segmentation measurements. At the endpoint, 77.51% of its nonzero spectral energy lies in the lowest measured radial shell, and an adaptive threshold covers 58.21% of the lattice. These describe low-frequency variation and a statistical partition, not an intrinsic biological length or distinct functional phases. The controlled same-process observer comparison also matches at all 41 checkpoints, although separate-run bitwise equivalence is not established.

## Poking the universe {.unnumbered}

G0028 imposes a localized event on one arm of a matched-seed pair. The verified pilot establishes a short-term, small-domain contrast. The longer 24⁴ experiment tests the same principle across 4,800 steps, with the event at step 2400. Its local energy deficit relative to control diminishes by 94.35%; the local matrix contrast decreases in magnitude by 88.05%, crossing from negative to positive.

That crossing is not equivalent to recovery of an original structure. The two arms finish with similar global mean matrix concentrations but different high-threshold coverage: 5.00% in the disturbed arm and 10.50% in control. The relative populations also diverge. The experiment therefore records both an attenuating local disturbance contrast and persistent differences in the wider state. These observations concern one seed, one intervention geometry and one numerical model.

G0028's moving images express a different dimension of the inquiry. They are interpretations of a single simulated world, not substitutes for the numerical comparison. The distinction between the visual effect of an event and the measured change in a field is central to reading the work responsibly.

## Where to take the contraption next {.unnumbered}

Further directions include adaptive transport, ecological succession, functional compartmentalization and longer-duration dynamics. A proposed mechanism is not a demonstrated outcome. In particular, a compartment should be examined through transport and metabolic function rather than inferred from a closed shape, and a persistent identity should not be confused with biological heredity without appropriate tests.

The project remains open-ended. Mathematical systems can resist an intended form, and their observed behavior can redirect the next question. Ontogenesis brings that resistance into a process of human direction, AI-assisted construction, numerical experiment and visual interpretation. What emerges is not only a series of images, but a method for exploring what can be made perceptible and what can responsibly be inferred.
