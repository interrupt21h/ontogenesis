# Final renders and visualization fidelity {.unnumbered}

A simulation image is both a record and an interpretation. The numerical field determines what may be shown; its visual presentation determines how the structure is encountered. The selected frames that follow document the changing visual vocabulary of Ontogenesis rather than a continuous trajectory of one artificial organism.

## From fields to spatial experience {.unnumbered}

The first experiments reveal pattern, competition and differentiation directly in the chemical concentrations. Transport and resources give those forms motion and persistence at different scales. Higher-dimensional experiments introduce a new problem: no single image can display an entire four-dimensional domain. A section, projection or moving viewpoint selects only part of that state.

G0024 investigates independent inherited construction traits. Its visual observatory makes differentiated chemistry and population activity legible, while the final matrix illustrates why appearance alone cannot establish structural persistence: four cells remain above threshold 0.20 even though roughly half the lattice exceeds threshold 0.15.

G0025 introduces energy-funded maintenance and supplies a particularly rich volumetric mode of observation. Emission–absorption rendering samples a three-dimensional section of its four-dimensional field. Light and color express the numerical variation without turning the sampled view into a complete representation of the world.

G0026 and G0027 turn attention toward observation itself. Their principal results are diagnostic: observer checks, distributional statistics, spectral energy and adaptive thresholds. These are meaningful experimental outcomes, although they need not produce a new cinematic universe.

G0028 introduces a localized environmental intervention. Its paired pilot measures the disturbed and untreated arms directly. The selected cinematic preview, by contrast, follows a single observed world: it makes an intervention visually perceptible but cannot by itself establish a causal recovery. Independently archived full-domain cinematic editions extend the available visual interpretations; this gallery preserves the source frames explicitly selected for this publication.

## Reading the frame plates {.unnumbered}

The following pairs of frames are identified by generation and **playback position**, not by inferred physical time. They were derived from separate numerical experiments and rendered using distinct observation methods. Color brightness and image sharpness should not be compared as common measures of population viability, structure or numerical precision.

The plates present successive artistic approaches to observing a system: structural specialization, volumetric maintenance and environmental disturbance. The corresponding experimental chapters provide the measurements and limitations required for scientific interpretation. The selected images are part of the artwork, not substitutes for the underlying numerical archives.

## The visual horizon {.unnumbered}

Further investigation can explore persistent transport networks, succession, functional compartmentalization and long-duration spatial organization. Those remain research directions, not reported outcomes. The central artistic challenge is to make increasingly complicated numerical behavior perceptible without inventing biological meaning or sacrificing the distinction between observed state and visual transformation.
