# Final renders and visualization fidelity {.unnumbered}

The images in this section are not a continuous biological history. They are selected observations from separately initialized numerical experiments with different rules, integration histories and imaging instruments. Their chronological arrangement documents an evolving artistic vocabulary: early chemical forms; later volume and identity observatories; increasingly discriminating matrix measurements; and finally the attempted visualization of a controlled environmental intervention.

The **final render** is a publication object, not an additional generation of numerical evolution. The individual stills below come from verified source animations and retain their exact workflow-artifact identifiers. They are not conceptual illustrations, composite reconstructions from the PDF, or measurements extracted from image colors.

## From simulated structure to visible form {.unnumbered}

G0001–G0003 establish pattern and contrast; G0004–G0011 give chemical fields motion, resources and signaling; G0012–G0017 move toward four-dimensional sections, visual identity and inherited variation. G0018–G0023 complicate that picture with structural barriers, topology, threshold dependence, and controlled construction comparisons. Their earlier plates remain in their individual chapters, rather than being silently reclassified as final G0028 outcomes.

The last numerically and visually developed sequence is **G0024–G0025**. G0024 adds independent construction traits; G0025 studies energy-funded maintenance. Their observatory views include evolving volume, measured chemistry and additional readouts. They form the project's established visual baseline, although their recorded concentrations do not by themselves establish functional organisms.

G0026 and G0027 intentionally produce mainly **measurement-based evidence**, not new artistic universes. G0026 verifies observer noninterference under tested conditions. G0027 examines matrix structure through spectrum and adaptive segmentation. Their quantitative diagrams are experiments in observation and are retained where those results are discussed; they are not missing frames of a narrative that supposedly had continuous cinematic output.

G0028 returns to the world with a matched intervention. Its verified **$12^4$ paired pilot** supports numerical comparison, and a separate **$12^4$ cinematic preview** demonstrates rendering technology. The latter is not a full-domain mature ecology: its chemical activity decays early. The longer **$24^4$ single-plane cinematic** and **multi-$\omega$ high-fidelity** runs are now separately completed and archived artistic editions. They have not been promoted into this report's curated gallery without visual review, nor do they substitute for the still-unreconciled **$24^4$ paired-control science experiment**. Their existence, source fidelity and limitations are documented in the [repository's visual gallery](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/blob/main/docs/VISUAL_GALLERY.md); their ecological effectiveness must not be inferred from the film.

## Reading the selected final frames {.unnumbered}

The next three plates show the intermediate and closing playback observations for G0024, G0025 and G0028. The time index on each plate is a **video playback frame**, not a simulation step, unless a numerically verified step is explicitly supplied. The images were captured with different methods; apparent sharpness or color intensity is not a scale-independent measure of ecological complexity. This gallery is an interpretation of the work as visual art, accompanied by numerical provenance, not a substitute for the actual films.

**G0024** shows the developing specialization observatory and its last recorded view. Its final matrix statistics have to be interpreted at explicitly stated thresholds: the G0024 endpoint has four cells above 0.20, despite substantial coverage at 0.15. A saturated color does not override that measurement.

**G0025** presents the maintenance-era observatory. Its volumetric emission/absorption renderer uses a 96-cubed ray grid to image a selected three-dimensional section of a four-dimensional simulated world. The stage-by-stage images are not independent samples of all four spatial dimensions.

**G0028** shows the verified *reduced-domain cinematic preview* rather than an unverified full-domain film. It records measured three-dimensional fields at one fixed $\omega$ coordinate, using animated voxel-based camera motion and display interpolation. A moving camera can be visually dynamic even after the numerical chemical activity diminishes. A separate multi-$\omega$, seven-field and ray-integrated edition has now passed its CI processing and retains the measured archive. Its visual merit remains a separate editorial question; the gallery still shows the previously inspected preview.

## A closing standard for subsequent cinematic editions {.unnumbered}

The project should not trade away G0025's spatial, temporal or perceptual fidelity merely to make a new generation look stylistically different. Featured releases must distinguish lattice resolution from encoded video size, list native observation cadence and numerical precision, and preserve source fields when practical. Simulation data, analytical diagrams, conceptual illustrations, and artistic rendering transforms must have distinct captions and provenance.

G0029–G0032 remain planned directions—adaptive transport, ecological succession, functional compartments and extended-universe dynamics—not additional completed visual experiments. This gallery therefore closes the **verified edition**, while keeping future artworks conceptually open.
