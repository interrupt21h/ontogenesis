# Generation 0035 — Morphogenic Tide

*Type: Derived, art-directed visualization experiment using the verified G0033 native field archive. This is not an additional numerical model integration.*

[← Experiments](../EXPERIMENTS.md) · [Visual gallery](../VISUAL_GALLERY.md) · [Art direction](../ART_DIRECTION.md)

## Premise

The recent Ontogenesis experiments have acquired three good ways of *explaining* numerical artificial-life-inspired structures: Contact Cartography maps four-dimensional interfaces; Temporal Loom compares recorded outcomes through time; and Lineage Ribbons diagrams connected-component genealogy. Morphogenic Tide starts with a different artistic question: **can actual stored numerical concentration contours feel like the environment of an unfamiliar computational ecosystem?**

This edition avoids another point cloud, dashboard or animated diagram. Its artistic language consists of luminous sculptural contour bands, large negative space, alternating cool and warm concentration fields, and a distinct three-act narrative built from matched numerical treatments.

## Authentic numerical source

The source is the successfully completed G0033 eight-seed native 4D interface pilot, restricted to the original **seed 73**, 16⁴ grid, 240 numerical integration steps and its **eleven genuinely recorded samples at steps 0, 24, 48, …, 240**. Both source treatments are present:

| Arm | Model parameter | Interpretation |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `separated_070` | Externally prescribed complementary niche strength 0.70 | Distinct spatial preferences |
| `near_transition` | No spatial niche | Matched no-niche control |

The original V, W and matrix arrays have shape `[11,16,16,16,16]`; the original compressed archive SHA-256 is checked against the independently published G0033 numerical summary before the renderer is allowed to publish.

## Morphology and artistic mapping

For every displayed source state, a *recorded* omega hyperplane is selected from the native 4D field, producing a 16×16×16 three-dimensional grid. The numerical `z` axis is reduced by a **maximum-projection** onto the actual 16×16 y/x lattice, independently for V, W and matrix. The z-max operator is a precise, reproducible scalar reduction of the original data. It is **not a simulated membrane or a native connected surface**.

The art film resamples the measured 16×16 projections into large visual panels using bicubic image-space interpolation. Over this presentation-only grid, four nested isobands emphasize concentration levels 0.12, 0.24, 0.42 and 0.64. Local gradient contrast, optical bloom and a soft studio background give the contours their luminous, tide-like material quality. Those effects affect only the rendered image.

- Cyan = presentation of the original V concentration.
- Amber = presentation of the original W concentration.
- Violet = presentation of the original matrix field.
- Camera/focus and lighting are editorial. No additional solver states, organisms, or causal fluid forces are invented.
- Temporal progression advances **only through recorded numerical steps**. Apparent visual fluidity between discrete states is an artistic treatment, not an interpolated simulation.

## Three-act cinematic structure

**Act I — The constructed habitat.** A large, atmospheric view of the complementary-niche field uses real recorded omega planes and numerical timepoints.

**Act II — A world without imposed niches.** The same source seed is replayed with the original matched no-niche treatment, retaining the same visualization approach.

**Act III — Two numerical fates.** A controlled side-by-side composition shows the treatments at the original terminal step 240, with a moving selection of recorded omega planes. Differences reflect the archived fields; the final arrangement is an artistic comparison.

## Companion evidence film

The matched **evidence edition** is deliberately unlike the art film. It shows the original **16×16 z-max lattice directly**, expanded with nearest-neighbor (non-smoothed) pixels, and overlays exact counts of V-active, W-active and jointly active native projected cells under threshold 0.10. No bicubic contours, light bloom or synthetic detail are present in the displayed data plane.

These statistics refer to the z-max **projected columns** at the selected omega plane, not the entire 16⁴ grid. The accompanying text and render manifest distinguish these from full-four-dimensional population metrics.

## What it does not demonstrate

Morphogenic Tide is computational cinema derived from artificial-life-inspired numerical fields, **not evidence of autonomous life**. It does not implement new cellular biology, real fluid transport, metabolic cooperation or evolutionary adaptation. No new independent simulation has been claimed for G0035. The same verified numerical data support two intentionally different visual interpretations so the spectacle can be examined instead of merely trusted.
