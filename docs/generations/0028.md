# Generation 0028 — Localized disturbance and recovery

Status: **G0028 pilot CI passed (12⁴, 240 steps); full 24⁴ study pending manually dispatched execution**.

## Research question and hypothesis

Can the established four-dimensional artificial ecosystem attenuate a localized environmental disturbance relative to an identical unperturbed control? A reduction in the post-event difference would be consistent with recovery of a particular observable, but is not proof of ecological resilience or biological function. The intervention is externally imposed, not an evolved adaptation.

## Equations and parameters

Let the system be a periodic $N^4$ lattice with integer coordinates and shortest wrapped Euclidean distance $d_{\mathrm{per}}$ to the lattice center. Define the dimensionless spatial perturbation mask:

$$
G(\mathbf{x})=\exp\left(
-\frac{d_{\mathrm{per}}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}_0)^2}{2\sigma^2}
\right),
\qquad \sigma=N/5.5.
$$

At $t_s=\lfloor T/2\rfloor$, remove fractions of the local energy and matrix once:

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+(\mathbf{x})&=E^-(\mathbf{x})\left[1-0.70G(\mathbf{x})\right],\\
M^+(\mathbf{x})&=M^-(\mathbf{x})\left[1-0.80G(\mathbf{x})\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

All other field equations remain unchanged. The regional core is $G\geq \exp(-1/2)$, corresponding to within one Gaussian width of the center. Removal fractions can be varied explicitly; the defaults are exploratory and not fitted.

## Initialization, lattice and numerical integration

Both arms use seed 42, identically initialized states and the same random update schedule. The event is applied *immediately before* the observation at the midpoint. Routine CI executes the paired $12^4$ / 240-step pilot, event step 120, with field recordings every 12 steps and at event-adjacent steps. The full $24^4$ / 4800-step paired experiment is manually dispatched, with 40-step recordings and event step 2400. The paired full experiment costs approximately two complete simulations; no GPU or non-GitHub resources are used.

## Rendering and output artifacts

[Intervention design schematic](../figures/0028.svg) — **conceptual design only, not measured simulation imagery**.

The workflow emits numerical diagnostics as JSON and an independent MP4 plus PNG plate generated from actual recorded concentrations. It captures native two-dimensional sections through the four-dimensional state at $\omega=z=N/2$, varying $y$ and $x$. Six panels compare control, disturbed and their difference, for matrix $M$ and energy $E$. Neither the panels nor their differences are reconstructed from video colors. Color scales are fixed for the entire film. Video frames are observations at explicitly recorded steps, with no invented/interpolated field states. When used, a display hold repeats an actual sample rather than inventing intermediate states.

The JSON includes the measured global means, local-core means, real section arrays, timing, model settings and historical 13-field state hashes. The primary comparisons are the paired differences in local matrix and local energy at the event and final step. For an observable with immediate and terminal paired differences $\Delta_s,\Delta_T$, attenuation is defined as

$$
A=1-\frac{|\Delta_T|}{|\Delta_s|},\qquad |\Delta_s|>10^{-12}.
$$

Negative attenuation means the paired difference *grew*; this is possible and must not be hidden. A positive value does not establish recovery of all fields or trajectories.

## Diagnostics, invariants and controls

The arms must share checkpoint hashes before the event and differ after the event. Measured means must be finite. Observer arrays are read-only views, preventing observer mutation without copying entire 4D volumes. The $12^4$ pilot tests execution and methodology but cannot establish large-domain outcomes. A second seed, a sham intervention, sensitivity sweeps and spatial-resolution tests would strengthen any later causal conclusion.

### A limitation of the apparent matrix recovery

At the pilot intervention, the unperturbed core matrix concentration was only about 0.005535 and was already falling; its final mean was approximately 0.002087, whereas the disturbed arm ended near 0.001724. Thus 89.09% *attenuation of the difference* is not restoration to the pre-event matrix level. Because both trajectories are in an early transient, the pilot should be understood as a strong procedural/control demonstration, not mature ecological resilience. The longer full-domain study applies its event at step 2400, after substantially more system evolution, but that experiment has not yet been executed.

## Interpretation and limitations

Single seed, a prescribed localized event, periodic edges, dimensionless concentrations, first-order transport, numerical discretization, time-limited observation and visual sections of a 4D volume. A physically coherent biological analogy is not established. Changes in global measures may be small even when the local slice shows a substantial effect. Evolutionary claims require stronger experimental controls.
