## A new numerical question

G0036 established that imposed, four-dimensional state-dependent transport feedback measurably changes the simulated populations. G0037 asks **whether its strength changes the persistence of recorded numerical structure**. It does not presume that increased transport feedback is beneficial.

Four independently initialized seeds, **42, 73, 101 and 137**, are run under three otherwise identical conditions:

| Arm | Structural feedback gain |
|:--|--:|
| Control | 0.0000 (original unchanged transport) |
| Low | 0.0175 |
| High | 0.0700 |

The experiment preserves the original four-dimensional 12⁴ grid, static complementary habitat strength 0.70, V decay multiplier 0.97, existing transport constraints and per-seed initialization. The high gain remains under the existing feedback-law bound of 0.08. Native V, W and matrix arrays are recorded at exactly **steps 0, 24, 48, 72, 96 and 120**, without restarting a historical field.

## Primary measurement

At successive stored sample times, the matrix concentration defines a four-dimensional set of threshold-active lattice coordinates:

\[
A_t=\{x:m(x,t)>0.15\}.
\]

The study computes exact native 4D mask overlap:

\[
J_t=\frac{|A_{t-24}\cap A_t|}{|A_{t-24}\cup A_t|}.
\]

The Jaccard coefficient describes **same-location structural persistence** between two recorded numerical snapshots. This does not track individual objects, organism memory, coherent motion or the unrecorded steps between samples. When both masks are empty, the coefficient is **undefined** (`null` in published JSON), not automatically 1.

Other measured readouts include native population activity, exclusive 4D contact faces, mean matrix concentration and mean energy. Endpoint full-field absolute differences from the unchanged control are retained for both feedback-strength variants. No favorable hypothesis is assumed in advance.

## Memory Strata cinematic concept

The new artistic film has its own visual grammar: **time-layered topographic ribbons**, not a rotating particle cloud, pseudo-cell animation, or another line-chart dashboard. The filmed structures come from distinct genuinely recorded numerical states. Each ribbon is formed by the spatial profile of a real 12×12 z-max concentration projection from a chosen original 4D omega hyperplane. Its position in the scene, lighting, depth and color are artistic staging.

Three acts reveal the high-feedback history for seed 42, the corresponding history for seed 73 and finally the three prescribed gains in matched final-state comparison. The companion **evidence film** displays exact nearest-neighbor 12×12 matrix projection cells for all three arms, with the directly computed full-4D native Jaccard score where defined.

Both 24-second, 1280×720 masters require verified native-field SHA-256 identities, sampled-time metadata, a smoke encode, complete H.264 decoding, distinct posters and clear declarations that numerical states were never interpolated.
