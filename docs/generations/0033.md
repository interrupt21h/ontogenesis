# G0033 — Is the interface real, robust, and persistent?

**Status: Implemented in GitHub Actions; execution and outcomes have not yet been verified.**

[Previous experiment: G0032](0032.md) · [Methodology](../METHODOLOGY.md) · [Native 4D interface implementation](../../niche_spatial_interface.py)

## Motivation

G0032 measured 2,806 periodic four-dimensional lattice faces shared by exclusively V-active and exclusively W-active cells at step 480 in one seed (73), under a fixed complementary habitat preference. Only two cells exceeded both population activity thresholds. The other measured seed did not reproduce joint global persistence. This makes the interesting result a **seed-dependent spatial interface**, not established ecological stability, cooperation, or an artificial organism.

G0033 should test whether that interface survives independent seeds, later observations, threshold choices, and explicitly defined negative controls before introducing another biological-sounding mechanism.

## Questions

1. Across multiple independent initialization seeds, how often do both active populations persist globally?
2. For runs with global joint activity, do exclusive population domains remain adjacent in the native 4D grid, and for how long?
3. Is the measured contact surface robust to activity thresholds, or is it mainly a consequence of the chosen 0.1 cutoff?
4. How much contact is expected from two density-matched masks after destroying their spatial arrangement?
5. Does the niche intervention change contact persistence relative to the matched no-niche control?

## Controlled protocol

- **Baseline:** retain the G0032 integrator, four-dimensional periodic lattice, fixed terrain, V-loss multiplier 0.97, and its existing control/niche arms. Do not silently change dynamics.
- **Initialization:** use at least eight independent, recorded seeds, including 42 and 73. Within each seed, verify that the no-niche and spatial-niche treatments begin from identical numerical states.
- **Primary feasibility stage:** 16⁴ lattice, **240 numerical steps**, with two treatments and eight independent seeds sampled every 24 steps. The 480-step extension is a separate future stage, not part of this bounded first execution.
- **Sensitivity:** recompute diagnostics from the same archived field samples for predeclared thresholds 0.05, 0.10, and 0.20. This is *post-processing*, not three newly evolved ecosystems.
- **Negative control:** generate 16 reproducible, four-class-count-preserving spatial-mask permutations per treatment endpoint. Describe clearly that this destroys spatial correlation and therefore only tests whether contact differs from a shuffled arrangement; it is not a mechanistic ecological counterfactual.
- **Primary outputs:** seed-by-treatment sampled interface trajectories and endpoint summaries; active population counts, joint global activity at the endpoint, contact faces and contact fraction per 4D face, both-active cells, continuous shared-mass ratio, and endpoint mask-permutation reference distributions.
- **Provenance:** record commit SHA, seed, grid size, step cadence, treatment parameters, archive SHA-256, threshold values, and the documented deterministic permutation-seed derivation. Never render reconstructed native structures from population-wide scalar summaries.

## Interpretation rules

Report the denominator for every proportion. Separate **joint global activity**, **same-cell activity**, **exclusive-region face contact**, and **continuous shared mass**. A change in thresholded contact does not imply metabolism or communication across a boundary. Avoid calling a 240-step observation stable coexistence, and never present camera motion or light effects as physical model dynamics.

Use side-by-side matched controls. If only seed 73 remains jointly active, identify the outcome as non-replicated rather than choosing a more attractive camera or extending a claim.

## CI and publication gates

1. Unit-test four-axis periodic face accounting, boundary wraparound, exact threshold behavior, and mass-overlap distinctions.
2. Keep the initial numerical pilot bounded by explicit job and artifact limits, and split simulation from optional cinematic rendering.
3. Verify source archive hashes and schema, reject NaNs/infinities, and record skipped or incomplete seeds.
4. Publish plots and downloadable numerical data **only after the runs pass**. Add the experiment to the 32-generation atlas and PDF only when real G0033 observations exist, and update its advertised generation count then.
5. Render a native-field cinematic only if genuine field snapshots were captured; if not, label any animation a measured-data visualization.

**Expected value:** distinguish a genuinely reproducible numerical spatial pattern from a visually striking but seed-specific threshold effect. Whatever the outcome, it can make the next generative-art edition more defensible without pretending the project is a scientific discovery.
