## How to read these images

The first 26 posters are genuine decoded frames from the selected historical movies; where a clip has no spatial state archive, do not confuse appearance with a preserved volume. G0027 is a **real numerical chart**, not a missing or invented movie. G0028 and G0029 are spatial presentations of archived simulation fields. G0030–G0032 instead connect saved time-series values in animated charts: these are **not spatial re-creations**.

The G0028 full-resolution cinematic remains the project's featured artwork. G0029's two alternative volumetric films are retained. The newer evidence editions do not replace the featured film or the cumulative evolution film.
