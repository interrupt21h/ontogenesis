# Ontogenesis — visual art direction and rendering contract

*Creative framework for numerical-world cinema, not a scientific research protocol.*

## The ambition

**Make the viewer feel that a strange, self-organizing numerical world is alive with possibility, without asserting that it contains biological life.** The centerpiece of each generation should be a *distinct, visually authored* short film, not an animated debug dashboard. Scientific measurements remain available as a separate, inspectable layer.

The closest reference is not a scientific PowerPoint: it is a speculative nature documentary about an unfamiliar computational ecology, with the restraint of gallery installation art. Structures can look like luminous reefs, fungal branching, liquid glass, signal filaments, electric membranes, or deep-sea bioluminescence. Those are **visual metaphors only**; they are never described as demonstrated cells, predators, genetics, or living organisms.

## Core principles

1. **Algorithm first.** Identify the generation's actual distinguishing operation: a field flowing, a front advancing, an interface separating, a component fragmenting, or a topology changing. That operation determines the visual grammar.
2. **An authored visual language.** Compose deliberate negative space, subject hierarchy, foreground/background depth, luminous material, slow camera behavior, and color contrast. Do not repeat the same 3D point cloud with a different hue.
3. **Show a world rather than a dashboard.** Film A may depict a cavernous luminous habitat or an evolving substrate; Film B may be a forensic atlas or evidence-rich companion. The cinematic edition should not be covered in metrics or boxes.
4. **Temporal honesty.** Generate movement from actual numerical evolution at stored steps, documented camera movement, an explicitly aesthetic light pass, or deterministic interpolation of *presentation* only. Never insinuate unstored solver states were simulated.
5. **Art and evidence are separate artifacts.** Publish the film and poster together with numerical measurements, field archive provenance, render metadata, and a concise interpretation of what the film does *not* demonstrate.
6. **No generic beauty contest.** A new film must expose a genuinely new mechanism, perspective, material treatment, or causal comparison. A mere palette change, camera orbit, or dashboard animation is not a new edition.
7. **Legibility at real viewing sizes.** Visual stories should work at 1280×720, remain compelling when reduced, and communicate the subject with the typography muted or hidden. Prefer limited onscreen text with any detail in a companion gallery.
8. **Aesthetic uncertainty is acceptable.** Eerie, unexpected, asymmetrical, sparse, even nearly lifeless frames can be more truthful and interesting than populating an empty model with invented creatures.

## Visual families (choose a distinctive one per generation)

| Family | Artistically guided language | Data mapping | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luminous Habitat** | Deep dark negative space; suspended translucent structures; layered bloom; intimate light, not neon everywhere | Recorded 4D concentration hyperplanes, independently highlighted active regions | Light is artistic; occupancy is native |
| **Morphogenic Tide** | Sculptural contour bands, growth-front silhouettes, cinematic parallax | Recorded concentration contour values and spatial gradients | No generated fluid dynamics beyond solver |
| **Filament Ecology** | Lace-like local interfaces and branching signal diagrams with light carried along real structures | Native connected-component surfaces, adjacency and measured overlaps | No signal transport unless solver measures it |
| **Nocturnal Genome** | Speculative chromosome-like patterns, nested shells, spatial hieroglyphs | Measured spatial invariants, motifs and symmetry diagnostics | Not a literal DNA or heredity claim |
| **Topological Memory** | Constellations, filaments, slow lineages and thresholded disappearance | Exact sampled component overlap graph | Not tracked biological individuals |
| **Differential Worlds** | Symmetrical split-screen comparison, common framing, mirrored controls | Identically sampled treatment runs and seeds | No selection of only photogenic evidence |
| **Field Cartography** | Multiple 4D hyperplanes, luminous cartographic surfaces | Actual native sampled hyperplanes | Distinguish projected planes from literal 4D sight |

## Cinematic sequencing

An edition should have a deliberate beginning, middle and end:

- **Invitation:** Establish scale, palette, negative space and one compelling numerical motif.
- **Revelation:** Follow a real transition, contact, fragmentation, or divergence that is specific to the algorithm.
- **Consequence:** End with an unmistakable difference from the opening, or honestly reveal that little changed.

Maintain a restrained film-specific title sequence, a subtle numeric source indicator, and a quiet concluding evidence card or end-frame metadata. Never let UI borders, labels or raw metrics obscure the main phenomenon.

## Technical contract

- Use real archived arrays or verified measured summaries. Keep SHA-256 identities in metadata.
- Expose the exact numerical sample index, sampled step, 4D hyperplane choice, thresholds, seed and control treatment where relevant.
- Separate *numerical state* from *display geometry*. A camera orbit, focus drift, bloom, motion trails and interpolation of brightness can be aesthetically continuous while the numerical underlying snapshots remain discrete.
- Match source hashes against the independent numerical result before rendering.
- Produce one high-quality master, a representative poster, and a render manifest with frame count, output dimensions, exact source hashes and the artistic transformations applied.
- Gate publication on independent numerical checks, short MP4 decode, full-frame decode, temporal variation, poster presence and visual-review notes.
- Keep a maximum runtime and predictable GitHub Actions artifact budget. Reuse previously verified numerical fields rather than rerunning expensive solvers for alternate camera/material treatments.
- Where lighting, shading or trails are fabricated by presentation, state so clearly. Do not label artistic shapes as organisms or biological structures.

## Review rubric

Score each submitted film from 0–5 on:

1. **Distinctiveness:** instantly recognizable apart from other Ontogenesis editions.
2. **Artistic coherence:** authored palette, composition, lighting and controlled framing.
3. **Algorithmic expressiveness:** the *unique* numerical mechanism is legible in the images.
4. **Cinematic movement:** genuine structural change and meaningful editorial movement, not just a spinning camera.
5. **Data integrity:** observed state and graphical embellishment are unambiguously distinguished.
6. **Production polish:** no text collisions, cramped panels, tiny subjects, encoding errors or misleading titles.

A film should aim for at least 4/5 on distinctiveness and expressiveness; CI validates correctness, not artistic taste. The review notes should be a human/editorial decision, not a falsely objective automatic score.

## Near-term development direction

1. Pair the G0034 overlap-genealogy analytics with a **Luminous Habitat** art film made from genuine 4D concentration fields. The graph remains forensic; the film evokes a place.
2. For the next numerical experiment, prefer a model intervention that produces observable feedback, morphology changes or a real divergence, rather than more scalar parameter sweeps.
3. Let the solver inspire entirely different visual metaphors, from liquid morphology and coral-like structures to luminous filaments and volumetric shadow. Never claim fabricated geometry is simulation output.

**Thesis:** Ontogenesis is an evolving art project that makes numerical artificial-life-inspired algorithms visually strange, engaging and memorable, with authentic evidence underneath the art.
