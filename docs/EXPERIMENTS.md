# Experiment atlas

[← Project overview](../README.md) · [Docs](README.md) · [Visual gallery](VISUAL_GALLERY.md) · [Measured history](HISTORY.md)

**Thirty-three independently initialized experiment designs** (G0001–G0033 at their individually documented numerical scopes), followed by **two derived visualization and observational studies (G0034–G0035)**, form an investigative chronology—not a continuous simulated organism. Each detailed record explains what was changed, what was measured, and what remains unverified. **CI verified** means the relevant workflow and checks completed; it does not by itself establish scientific validity.

## I — Chemical differentiation

G0001–G0005 explore pattern formation, diversification, symmetry, dynamics and feedback.

## II — Transport, energy and signaling

G0006–G0011 develop spatial volume, circulation, energetic constraints, communication and adaptive behavior.

## III — Higher dimensions and inheritance

G0012–G0017 introduce four-dimensional observation, overlap-based entity identities, heredity and ecosystem rules.

## IV — Structure, persistence and controls

G0018–G0023 investigate morphology, compartment-like structure, topology, persistence and factorial niche controls.

## V — Structural specialization

G0024–G0025 explore independent construction and energy-funded maintenance; G0025 is the established volumetric visual-quality baseline.

## VI — Measurement and instrumentation

G0026 and G0027 focus on observer invariance and quantitative structure. They are meaningful numerical experiments even without new cinematic universes.

## VII — Environmental intervention

G0028's **12⁴ pilot** and [full 24⁴, 4,800-step matched-control experiment](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/44840239338) are verified. The larger experiment observes 94.35% attenuation of the local energy deficit and a sign change in the matrix contrast; disturbed high-threshold matrix coverage remains lower (5.00%) than control (10.50%). Both separately rendered 24⁴ artistic masters also passed CI, but their movies are not the two-arm causal measurement.

## VIII — Transport with memory

G0029 adds a memory-driven curl correction to the four-dimensional G0026 circulation. Its 12⁴ pilot and [24⁴, 4,800-step matched full run](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45232504379) both passed CI. Unlike the near-inactive small pilot, the full adaptive arm ends with **89.01% active W versus 68.28% control**, but only **0.21% active V versus 3.61% control**, and lower matrix coverage (4.73% versus 10.50%). These measured redistributions do not imply an ecological improvement.

## IX — Ecological controls

G0030's [two-seed 16⁴ factorial pilot](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45266523722) passed CI. In all eight endpoints V coverage above the activity threshold was 0%; W remained active with 68.55–87.17% coverage. The four cross-feeding treatments produced measurable differences but no thresholded co-occupancy or demonstrated ecological succession. [Posthoc analysis](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/releases/download/atlas-media/g0030-analysis.md) established V activity last above threshold at observation 30 and continuously below it from 60 onward—long before the event at 600. A [verified shorter viability screen](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45297793723) found V inactive from step 60 (seed 42) and step 36 (seed 73) under both no forcing and early supply reversals; the tested changes did not restore coexistence. Larger runs remain gated.

## X — Viability frontier

G0031's [verified matched 16⁴ pilot](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45332031263) tested five externally selected V loss-rate multipliers. **No treatment maintained endpoint active co-occupancy.** Seed 42 switched from W dominance to V dominance between 1.00 and 0.95, while seed 73 switched between 0.95 and 0.90. The model's numerical sensitivity points to competitive exclusion, not a demonstrated functional ecosystem; compartments remain a later possibility.

## XI — Complementary spatial niche controls

G0032's [verified 16⁴ pilot](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45335396705) found **both V and W active globally for seed 73** when static complementary habitat preferences were enabled, even though active same-cell co-occupation remained zero. Seed 42 did not replicate that outcome. Its measured habitat-centroid separation supports spatial differentiation under the externally imposed preference, **not** durable coexistence or adaptation. The [step-480 independently rerun comparison](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45338017117) found both populations globally active in seed 73's stronger niche treatment (V 64.26%, W 22.84%) but not in seed 42, and a tiny same-cell overlap (~2 cells). Actual 4D states have now been [archived and visualized](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45407767981) for seed 73 in a separate spatial film. A [verified native 4D contact analysis](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45410140099) measured **2,806 V-only/W-only lattice-face contacts** but only two co-occupied cells at step 480 for the seed-73 strong-niche arm. This does not prove functional mutualism. More seeds and longer tests are needed to establish durable behavior.

## XII — Four-dimensional contact cartography and replication

G0033's [verified eight-seed 16⁴, 240-step native-field screen](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45437009050) measured threshold-sensitive population activity and spatial interface faces under two matched externally specified niche conditions. The [G0033 Contact Cartography film](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/releases/download/atlas-media/ontogenesis-0033-contact-cartography.mp4) shows the actual four-dimensional contact topology across all 16 recorded omega hyperplanes for seeds 42 and 73; the [interactive Interface Lab](https://interrupt21h.github.io/ontogenesis/interface-lab.html) explores the complete eight-seed endpoint evidence. Results remain finite model diagnostics, not organism-level ecological claims.

## XIII — Four-dimensional component genealogy

G0034 is the first verified **derived algorithmic analysis**, rather than a new model integration. The [16⁴ native periodic four-axis connected-component study](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45459564925) uses the original G0033 field archives for seeds 42 and 73, with 11 actual stored steps and two matching habitat interventions. Components are face-connected across all four periodic axes; an observed overlap edge is created only when component masks share actual native lattice sites at adjacent stored times. The [Lineage Ribbons film](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/releases/download/atlas-media/ontogenesis-0034-lineage-ribbons.mp4) and [interactive Observatory](https://interrupt21h.github.io/ontogenesis/lineage-observatory.html) make these discrete graph relationships visible without calling them organism births, deaths or biological lineages.

## XIV — Art-directed numerical morphology: G0035

G0035 [Morphogenic Tide](generations/0035.md) is the second expressly **derived** recent experiment. It repurposes true recorded G0033 four-dimensional fields as sculptural isoband cinema instead of running a new integrator. The [**art film**](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/releases/download/atlas-media/ontogenesis-0035-morphogenic-tide-art.mp4) draws luminous concentration contours under cinematic lighting. The [**evidence edition**](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/releases/download/atlas-media/ontogenesis-0035-morphogenic-tide-evidence.mp4) displays the same sampled source as exact 16×16 z-max projected grids. Both passed the [source-hash and decoded-video workflow](https://github.com/interrupt21h/ontogenesis/actions/runs/45480155128), and the [G0030–G0035 release audit](../.github/workflows/recent-cinematic-coverage.yml) verifies published MP4/poster pairs for all six recent generations.

## Planned, not completed

G0030's verified pilot did not demonstrate succession. G0031's verified screen found a sharp competitive-dominance switch; G0032 showed that seed 73 can retain globally active but spatially separate populations under externally imposed complementary niches. **Replication across additional seeds, stable niche persistence, functional compartmentalization and the extended-universe finale** are not yet demonstrated. G0033's eight-seed 240-step replication screen has passed CI, but does not establish indefinite stability or artificial organism behavior. G0034 and G0035 are derived numerical visualizations, not new autonomous-life experiments. Nothing yet demonstrates stable autonomous life. The verified G0029 cinematic editions remain distinct from measured numerical evidence.
