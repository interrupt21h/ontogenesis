Interpretation

Components use nearest-neighbor connectivity across all four periodic lattice dimensions. Adjacent sampled components are linked only when they contain the same native lattice sites. Split and merge events are defined by the degrees of this discrete overlap graph.

These are numerical threshold crossings and changes in recorded connectivity. A feature can disappear between samples, migrate, or temporarily fall below threshold without a unique persistent identity. Such ambiguities must not be interpreted as organism lineage.
