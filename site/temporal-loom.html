Reading this experiment

Every square corresponds to a recorded measurement from one independently initialized numerical simulation. Each grid traverses 11 actual observations: steps 0, 24, 48, …, 240. All four simulation axes are included when calculating periodic exclusive V/W contact faces.

The two treatments share the same eight initialization seeds. Habitat preferences are prescribed externally. The observations cannot establish organisms, metabolism, biological communication or indefinite ecological stability.
